Papyrbound transforms the JSON export into alphabetic lookup indexes, retaining
definitions, part of speech, pronunciations, up to two examples per sense and
inflected forms. Semantic relations and other lexical metadata are omitted.
Build instructions and the source archive checksum are included in
scripts/build-dictionary.py and public/dictionary/metadata.json.
